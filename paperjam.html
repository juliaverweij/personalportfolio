    PAPERJAM

    PAPERJAM is a collaborative project that aims to open up a platform for students to sell their creative work at a fair price. This project started from a mutual excitement of wanting to sell our work and wanting to start building a network for ourselves. Previously this translated into a website and currently we are going physical with a market.

    PAPERJAM is currently busy organizing an art & book market which will take place December 4th 2026. For more information check out our website www.studiopaperjam.com
